Save bar

.save-bar holds a long form's status line, Discard and Save. It sticks to the bottom of the window while the form is on screen and settles under the last field at the end. Scroll this page to see it.

On the page

The last child of the <form>. The role="status" line takes the leading space; on a narrow screen it moves to a line of its own above the buttons, and while it is empty it takes no line at all.

Unsaved changes

<form class="grid gap-4" novalidate @submit.prevent="save">
  <!-- fields -->
  <div class="save-bar -mx-gap px-gap">
    <p role="status">{{ status }}</p>
    <button type="button" class="btn outlined" :disabled="!form.isDirty || form.processing"
        @click="discard">Discard</button>
    <button class="btn primary" :class="{ loading: form.processing }" :disabled="form.processing"
        :aria-busy="form.processing">Save</button>
  </div>
</form>

In a card, with nothing to say

On a card, the bar takes the card's colour with [--save-bar-bg:var(--color-card)]. An empty status line still pushes the buttons to the end.
