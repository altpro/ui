Checkbox, radio, switch

.checkbox, .radio and .switch restyle the native input. No JavaScript, no extra elements: the box is the input and the mark is its ::before.

Two ways to label

Put the class on the <input> when the label sits beside it with for, or on a <label> that wraps the input. The wrapped form is shorter and the whole label is the click target.

<input class="checkbox" id="terms" type="checkbox"> <label for="terms">I agree</label>

<label class="checkbox"><input type="checkbox"> I agree</label>

Checkbox

Checked and indeterminate fill with the primary colour. indeterminate is a DOM property, so it is set from script. Invalid is aria-invalid="true", or a required box left unticked after the user has touched it.

<label class="checkbox"><input type="checkbox" checked> Checked</label>
<label class="checkbox sm"><input type="checkbox" aria-invalid="true"> Invalid</label>

Radio

Always round, whatever --radius is. Same states and sizes as the checkbox.

<label class="radio"><input type="radio" name="plan" checked> Free</label>
<label class="radio"><input type="radio" name="plan"> Team</label>

Switch

A checkbox with role="switch", so screen readers say on and off rather than checked. Use it for a setting that takes effect at once; a checkbox for something submitted with a form.

<label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" checked> Email me about updates</label>

In a field

A group is a <fieldset class="field"> with a <legend>. Hints and errors work as for any field; point aria-describedby at them from the inputs. A lone switch or checkbox can sit in a field with no label of its own.
