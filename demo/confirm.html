Confirm dialog

ConfirmDialog.vue is a .dialog that asks before an action and holds still while it runs. A button with command="show-modal" opens it. While busy, Esc, the cross and Cancel do nothing, and the confirm button keeps focus with its spinner.

Delete

tone="danger" for an action that can't be undone. Focus starts on Cancel, so Enter does not delete. This one takes a second and a half: try Esc while it runs.

<button class="btn outlined danger" command="show-modal" commandfor="delete-product">
  Delete product
</button>
<ConfirmDialog ref="dialog" id="delete-product" title="Delete Arc studio?"
    description="It goes from the shop at once. This can't be undone."
    confirm-label="Delete product" tone="danger" :busy="form.processing" @confirm="destroy" />

With a field

The default slot is the body, inside the dialog's own form, so Enter in the field confirms. autofocus on the field puts focus there instead of on Cancel. A wrong password keeps the dialog open with the error under the field; @close clears it. The password is password.

<ConfirmDialog ref="dialog" id="delete-account" title="Delete your account?" description="…"
    confirm-label="Delete account" tone="danger" :busy="form.processing" @confirm="destroy"
    @close="form.reset(); form.clearErrors()">
  <Field v-model="form.password" name="password" type="password" autocomplete="current-password"
      :errors="form.errors" required autofocus />
</ConfirmDialog>

Not destructive

The default tone is primary. The #description slot takes markup where the prop takes text.

<ConfirmDialog id="send-invoice" title="Send invoice INV-2026-0091?" confirm-label="Send invoice"
    :busy="form.processing" @confirm="send">
  <template #description>
    It goes to <strong>accounts@example.com</strong> with the PDF attached.
  </template>
</ConfirmDialog>
