Stat

A pattern, not a class. A .card holding a muted label, a big number in tabular-nums, and an optional change line. Put several in a grid gap-gap.

A row of stats

The label is a p, not a heading, so a dashboard of twelve stats does not become twelve sections. The change line says which way in words as well as colour, and the period it compares against.
