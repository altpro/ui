Avatar

.avatar on an <img>, or on a <span> holding initials, plus a hue and a size. Always a circle, whatever the radius knob says.

Images

Sizes xs sm lg xl, default 2.5rem. The last one wraps the image in a span, which works the same and lets you drop the image later without changing the class.

<img class="avatar lg" src="…" alt="Dev Patel">

Initials and hues

AO BS CM DP EL FB GH HK IJ JN KR LW MA NT OC PY QZ RD

Every Tailwind hue name is a modifier. No hue is neutral grey. Each is a tint in light and a deep shade in dark, and the text colour is derived from the fill so it meets 4.5:1 either way.

<span class="avatar blue">LW</span>
<span class="avatar pink lg">QZ</span>

Sizes

xs sm md lg xl

Groups

Inside .join, avatars overlap by a third of their width and get a 2px ring in the page colour. Later members sit on top. A plain avatar with a count makes the overflow marker.

On a surface that is not the page background, set --avatar-ring on the group so the ring matches.
