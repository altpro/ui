Tile

.tile is a <label> drawn as a card round one checkbox or radio, for a choice that needs a sentence or a picture. The whole tile is the click target. The input stays visible, so a reader can tell "pick one" from "pick any".

Radios and checkboxes

Checked draws the edge in the accent and tints the tile. Disabled fades the whole tile. The tile takes the focus ring; Tab lands on the input inside it.

A picture first

The input can sit anywhere inside the tile. flex-col stacks it under a picture; the picture is aria-hidden, as the label's words name the choice.

Colour scheme

Invalid

aria-invalid="true" on the inputs turns every tile's edge and ring to danger. The message goes under the group, as for any .field.
