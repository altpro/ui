Segmented control

A pattern, not a class. A .join of label.btn, each wrapping a radio. The checked label takes the solid look. No script: arrow keys move between radios and the form submits the value.

Colours and styles

Use outlined or ghost members so the checked one stands out. A solid colour has nowhere to go.

<div class="join" role="radiogroup" aria-label="Sort">
  <label class="btn outlined"><input type="radio" name="sort" checked> Newest</label>
  <label class="btn outlined"><input type="radio" name="sort"> Oldest</label>
  <label class="btn outlined"><input type="radio" name="sort"> Top</label>
</div>

Sizes and icons

Sizes are the button sizes. An icon-only member puts its aria-label on the input, since that is what screen readers read.

Multiple

Checkboxes instead of radios: any number can be on. The group is role="group".
