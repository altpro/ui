Local date

LocalDate.vue shows a date as the reader's calendar has it: "Today", "Yesterday", then "4 Nov", with the year once it is not this one. The words come from the reader's clock and timezone, so they are drawn after mount and change at the reader's midnight.

Days, and times where they matter

A list shows the day. Record facts and history add the time with with-time. In a sentence, in-sentence lowercases "today" and "yesterday". A missing value shows the default slot.

<LocalDate :value="order.created_at" />
<LocalDate :value="entry.created_at" with-time />
Last login <LocalDate :value="user.last_login_at" with-time in-sentence>never</LocalDate>.
