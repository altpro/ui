Nav

.nav on a <nav> makes a vertical menu out of plain links, <details> sections and headings. The sidebar on this site is one.

Links, icons, current page

Dashboard Projects Team Billing
Home Inbox 12 Team Settings

Icons are Lucide, inline, size-4, with aria-hidden. A trailing badge gets ml-auto. Mark the current page with aria-current="page".

<nav class="nav" aria-label="Main">
  <a href="/" aria-current="page"><svg class="size-4" …></svg> Home</a>
  <a href="/inbox"><svg class="size-4" …></svg> Inbox <span class="badge primary sm ml-auto">12</span></a>
</nav>

Collapsible sections

A <details> with a <summary> is a section. No JavaScript. open starts it expanded. The chevron is CSS.

</details>
