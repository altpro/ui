Page heading

The top of an app page, as Vue components for Inertia apps. PageHeading.vue is the page's one h1, with the breadcrumb above it on a page reached from another, and ListHeader.vue is a list page's title row: the count, Clear, the primary action and stat cards that filter. Both give the h1 tabindex="-1", so the app layout can move focus to it after a visit.
