Accordion

.accordion on a div holding native <details>. The summary is the trigger, the one element after it is the panel. It slides open and closed, and the chevron turns, with no script.

Exclusive group

Is it accessible?
Yes. A native disclosure: the summary is a button to screen readers, Space and Enter toggle it, and the open state is announced.
Is it styled?
Yes. It comes with default styles that match the other components, and Tailwind utilities override any of it.
Is it animated?
Yes. The panel slides on a grid row and the chevron turns. Both stop under prefers-reduced-motion.

The same name on each item makes the group exclusive: opening one closes the rest. That is the browser, not altpro/ui.

<div class="accordion">
  <details name="faq" open>
    <summary>Is it accessible?</summary>
    <div>Yes. A native disclosure.</div>
  </details>
  <details name="faq">
    <summary>Is it styled?</summary>
    <div>Yes.</div>
  </details>
</div>
