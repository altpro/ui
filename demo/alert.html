Alert

.alert on a div. An optional leading svg is the icon, a heading is the title, a p is the body. Colour words match the button. The text stays the page foreground so it reads on the tint in both schemes.

Colours

Neutral

No colour word. A bordered box on the card colour.

A brand-coloured note.

Success

Warning

Danger

The deploy failed. Production is still on v2.3.9.
